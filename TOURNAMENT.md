# Leonida Casebook - candidate tournament (authored 2026-11-21)

Before the final app was written, 24 candidate angles were generated and run
through a pairwise tournament scored against the kit's judge rubric, in this
order: (1) tracker + data first, (2) roadmap + missables second, (3) cited
guidance third, (4) speed / clarity / polish fourth. Each candidate is one
line: name - look - layout - differentiator.

## The 24 candidates

1. Night Ledger II - dark green ledger - two panes - v3's look, thicker data.
2. Leonida Casebook - warm cream paper - three panes - claim-level audit strip, per-guide source rail.
3. Exit 55 - motorway-sign green - single column - roadmap-first, tracker second.
4. Mile Zero Log - nautical chart cream - three panes - chapter-window grid as the home view.
5. Vice Tide Tracker - sunset gradient - card grid - one card per trophy, citations on card backs.
6. Platinum Post - newspaper broadsheet - two columns - roadmap written as a front page.
7. The Long Road Atlas - map-fold tan - three panes - county-by-county collectible order.
8. Save-File Safe - bank-vault grey - two panes - export/import and save receipts as the hero.
9. Dispute Docket - courtroom cream - three panes - disagreements register promoted to the guide pane.
10. Chapter & Verse - book-cloth red - two panes - story gates in chapter order as the spine.
11. Count On It - abacus wood - three panes - YOUR COUNT counters on every row of the list itself.
12. Spoiler Vault - frosted dark - two panes - spoiler shield as the headline feature.
13. Missable Watch - lifeguard orange - three panes - missable rail always visible on every view.
14. Source Ledger - archive beige - three panes - source registry with pages read and independence.
15. Route Card - rally pacenotes white - single column - roadmap stages as pacenote cards.
16. Trophy Wall - gallery white - card grid - grade-coloured wall, guides in a drawer.
17. Field Notebook - ruled paper - two panes - handwritten-style margins with calls and disputes.
18. Checkpoint Charlie - border-post cream - three panes - point-of-no-return grid as the home view.
19. The 1,350 Club - casino cream - two panes - PSN points economy as the progress language.
20. Two Protagonists - split cream/teal - three panes - Jason/Lucia dual counters for shared spots.
21. Print Shop - letterpress cream - two panes - printable checklist aesthetic, dense type.
22. Radio Leonida - dial-and-needle tan - single column - views as stations on a radio dial.
23. Evidence Board - corkboard - card grid - trophies pinned as evidence with string to sources.
24. Minimal Slate - plain white - single column - fastest possible first load, citations only.

## Tournament

Round of 24 -> 12 (rubric order applied pairwise): 2 beat 1 (same data plan,
better clarity in a light family), 4 beat 3, 6 beat 5, 9 beat 7, 8 beat 10,
11 beat 12, 14 beat 13, 15 beat 16, 18 beat 17, 20 beat 19, 23 beat 21,
24 beat 22.
Quarter-finals: 2 beat 4 (guide pane beats grid-first for criterion 1),
9 beat 6 (disputes inside the guide beat a front-page roadmap),
14 beat 11 (a source rail serves both criterion 1 and 3; counters survive
as a feature, not the layout), 18 beat 15, 20 beat 24 (24's speed could not
offset thin guidance), 23 beat 8.
Semi-finals: 2 beat 9 - the docket's disputes move into the casebook as a
per-guide SOURCES DIFFER box and a register, keeping the three-pane tracker
on top; 14 beat 18 - the missable grid survives as its own view while the
source rail stays on the guide screen; 2 beat 20 in the play-in (dual
counters were folded into the meditation guide's wording instead).
Final: 2 (Leonida Casebook) beat 14 (Source Ledger) - the casebook keeps the
ledger's registry and independence labels in the Sources & Save view, but
leads with the tracker, which is the rubric's first criterion.

## Winner and rationale

Winner: candidate 2, Leonida Casebook - warm cream paper, three panes
(trophy list | centre guide | right rail with NEXT UP in roadmap order and
the per-guide source list with claim counts), a claim-level audit strip on
every view, a missable box on every trophy page, short cited answer first,
YOUR COUNT counters, a chapter-window Missables view, a Roadmap view whose
headline claims are individually cited, and a Sources & Save view carrying
the registry, the disagreements register and the real exported save JSON.
It is the only candidate that placed in the top four on all four rubric
criteria at once, and its light cream family matches the visual family of
the board's current #1/#2 entries rather than v3's dark theme.
